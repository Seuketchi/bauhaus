# moholy-nagy

## What it does

`moholy-nagy` is the shot and motion designer: the shot, the frame and the move. It reads the brief, the project's `DESIGN.md` and the project's own rules, then hands back a plan: a shot list with a marker on every shot, a storyboard of framing panels with a rough animatic, or a short animated title, graphic or effect with its poster frame.

It calls five tools, each on a stated fact: `design` when no brief file exists, `shots` when the task is the shots of a film or a sequence, `storyboard` when the task is the frames of a film in order, or an animatic, `motion` when the task is an animated title, graphic or effect, and `critique` when a finished frame exists that this session did not make. The default is not to call.

Its stance is short and plain: it treats light as the material of the work, takes an unusual angle as a way to see and not as an error, builds a film from tempo, tension and tone, treats design as an attitude of planning, and refuses a picture made only to copy what the eye expects.

The work is a plan and a draft. No film is made, no shot is taken and no footage is cut, and every hand-over says so. Each scene states its axis in one line, and a shot that crosses it is flagged for the owner. A still photograph goes to the photographer, `peterhans`, and the shot list holds light only as a note.

The split is fixed. The writer owns the words of the pieces made here, donna keeps long documents and the PDF build, `bayer` takes brand, logos and email, `breuer` takes app and web screens, `mies` takes form, plans and structure, and louis reviews.

## When to reach for it

- A film or a sequence needs its shots planned before anything is shot.
- The frames of a film need to be seen in order, or played as a rough animatic.
- An animated title, graphic or effect is wanted as a short draft with a poster frame.
- A finished frame from another session needs a craft pass.

Skip it for brand, logos, email, app and web screens, buildings and spaces, still photographs and document text.

## Common questions

**Is the result a film?** No. It is a plan and a draft, not a cut, and the hand-over says so.

**What happens when a shot crosses the axis?** The row says so in its note and the hand-over lists it. The owner decides whether the crossing stays.

**Does it mark its own work?** No. The craft pass runs on a frame another session made, and it is not a sign-off.

**Is this a person speaking?** No. The register is plain and professional, and nothing in the file imitates a real person's voice or attributes words to anyone.

## It's working if

- Every hand-over carries the plan-and-draft line, and no piece is called a film or a cut.
- Every figure in a hand-over equals what the app printed.
- Every scene has its axis in one line, and every crossing is named.
- It calls a tool only for the stated trigger, and says in one line which tool and why.
