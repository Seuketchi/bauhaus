# The writer and the cast

Date: 2026-11-07

## Context

Nobody owns the idea, the insight, the story or the words of a piece. Two email trials showed it: both pieces failed first on the story, and the words were left as slots for someone outside the package. The owner wants the flow to run as questions to the owner, research, a concept and a story, craft and a critique pass, with a writer upstream beside the lead.

The cast so far are people of the school. The plan was to name the writer after Kállai, the editor of the school's journal in its last years, behind a gate: that name ships only if a source pass finds a usable method for writing in his texts. The pass opened the one full text of his that could be had, about 280 words, and found a stance about what a journal is for, not a method for an idea, a story or a line. The two named pieces sit in PDFs that were not opened because the host's robots rules keep readers out. The gate failed by default on no usable method found, and the pass does not claim more than that. It is not proof that he has none.

## Decision

- **The writer is Ogilvy.** His memo on plain writing is an open, written method: write the way people speak, short words, no jargon, read it aloud the next morning and edit, say plainly what the reader should do. It is the only open craft text about writing among the writers considered.
- **A theme change.** The cast is no longer only people of the school: the writer is from outside it. This record, a row in the casting table of section 4 of the spec and a row in the README cast table are the change. The person is no longer living, which keeps the statement that the cast are real people who have died true; the year is 1999, read from the encyclopedia page, which is a lead and not a primary source. No character file or docs page states it.
- **What the name gives.** A method for the words, from the memo, and a stance for ideas, from a second opened text: a letter of 1947 on persuasion as an art and not a science. The tool `concept` rests on those two writers' opened texts and on three more opened pages, on the idea cut to two words, on questions put to a client and on research before craft. Every row is in `skills/tools/concept/REFERENCES.md`, and a rule rests only on a row marked `opened`. The research rule does not cite Ogilvy, because the rows about research in his name were read from summaries, not from his text.
- **A tool of its own.** The writer gets `concept`, with a `REFERENCES.md`, because a craft's rules live in a tool and a character never copies a tool's text.
- **Rules for a real person.** Craft only, no biography, no year, no words put in his mouth; his work is paraphrased and never quoted, and his name is a name and nothing more. The character file and the docs page describe the craft in a plain professional register, and the tests that scan every character file for a cast name beside a speech verb, a quoted sentence or an avoided topic include the surname.
- **The words.** The writer owns the words of the pieces made here. Donna keeps long documents and the PDF build, which this package does not touch. Screen strings stay in the project's own l10n shape, and the project's rules win.

| Person | Role | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| David Ogilvy | Writer | The author of a written method for plain words, the one open craft text about writing among those considered. A writer from advertising, not of the school, so the fit is by craft only. The idea side rests on a second writer's letter, a stance and not a procedure. |

## Consequences

- The rights position of ADR 0001 applies unchanged: the repo is private with no releases, and a public release re-opens the question of the name and of the registers that describe other makers' work.
- Naming a real person in a character file is cheap to change while the repo is private and costly once public.
- The research rows about Ogilvy that rest on summaries stay out of the package until his own text is opened. Lending copies of his books would turn them into opened rows.
- The brief grows five required sections and old-shape briefs fail the check. No brief file is kept in this repo, so nothing here breaks.
- A name other than Ogilvy would be a new decision: the rules in the writer's tool that rest on the memo would rest on that person's opened rows instead.
